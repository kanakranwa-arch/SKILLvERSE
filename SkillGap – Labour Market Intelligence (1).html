SkillGap

    ·

    Load your own labour-market data. One line per skill and region:

    skill; region; monthly demand history (3+ numbers); supply per month

    Skill extraction from job-posting text. Paste postings separated by a blank line or a line of ---.

    Extraction here is rule-based matching against a built-in taxonomy of 12 skills. It does not call an external AI model. Use the counts as one month's demand signal when building history.

    Method: linear trend on your monthly history, 12-month average forecast, 80% range from the fit's residuals, gap = forecast − supply (shortage above +10% of supply, surplus below −10%). Statistics support decisions; people review them.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 13 October 2026 · English only

    What this is

    SkillGap is a non-commercial student prototype built for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

    What we collect, and why

    Guests: nothing is sent to us. Your work stays in this browser tab.

    Accounts: an email address and a password, only so you can log in. We also keep your chosen plan and your workspace (the data you load and your review decisions). We do not ask for your name, phone number, location or date of birth. The password is stored only as a salted hash.

    Where it is stored

    In your own browser (local storage), on this device only. It is not uploaded to any server we run, so we cannot see it, and it cannot be recovered if you clear your browser data.

    Team details

    The Team section of the home page shows the six team members' names, stream and contact details (email and mobile), revealed when a member is tapped. Any visitor can see them.

    Age

    Accounts are for people aged 18 or older, and you confirm this when creating an account. Anyone younger can use guest mode, which collects no personal data. We do not store your age.

    Third parties

    Google Fonts is the only third-party service, and it loads only if you allow it in the cookie banner. When allowed, your browser requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. This page is delivered by Claude's artifact hosting, which may keep standard server logs.

    Emails

    We do not send emails. If that ever changes, you will be asked to opt in first and every email will carry an unsubscribe link.

    Your rights and deletion

    You can export your data, clear your workspace, delete your account, or erase everything this site stored in your browser from the Data & deletion page. These actions take effect immediately.

    Changes

    If a server or any new data collection is added, this policy will be updated first.
